Ability ideas:
 - Shadow Swap
 	- Trade places with my shadow. Could make fun use of light mechanics.
 - Freeze Time
 	- We can freeze time for a certain period. Everything stops, but the player can continue.
 - Gravity Flip
 	- Reverse gravity, so the roof is now the floor and vice versa.
 - Teleportation
 	- Our hero throws this "core". Once the core lands, the hero is teleported to that place.
 - Size manipulation
 	- Grow/Shrink. Small, normal, large. Small = get through cracks/tiny holes. Large = destroy walls?
 - Momentum Dash
 	- Dashes to a side.
 - Dimension Toggle
 	- Swap between two dimensions of the same level.
 - Rewind
 	- Rewind yourself for 3 seconds.
 - Double Jump
 	- Self explanatory.
